Purpose

School Mail Bot helps the Gmail owner and their family understand selected school emails by preparing Russian summaries and delivering them to a configured Telegram group. It is not a public service and does not offer self-service registration for outside users.

Connection and recipients

Only connect a mailbox for which you are authorized to grant access. The owner chooses the senders, report recipients and family group membership. Connecting the mailbox authorizes the processing through Google, OpenAI, AWS and Telegram described in the Privacy Policy.

Checking the summaries

Summaries and translations are generated by artificial intelligence and may contain errors or omissions. Check deadlines, amounts, school requirements and other important information against the original email. The bot does not pay invoices, make decisions on the family's behalf or modify calendar events. Attachments are shown by filename; text inside those files is not summarized.

Schedule and availability

Report preparation starts daily at 09:00 in the Nicosia time zone. Actual delivery depends on message volume and the availability of external services. Delays, failures and occasional duplicate messages may occur after network errors. The default reporting period is the 24 hours preceding the scheduled start time.

Costs and stopping the application

Connecting Gmail does not purchase a subscription to this application. The owner of the selected API and infrastructure accounts is responsible for their providers' charges. Contact the bot owner to stop the schedule or remove the application. Gmail access can also be revoked independently in Google account settings.

Contact

For questions about operation, stopping processing or deleting data, contact the bot owner in the family group or use the support email shown on Google's consent screen. School Mail Bot is not an official product of Google, OpenAI or Telegram.
