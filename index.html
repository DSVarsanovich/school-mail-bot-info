A personal family assistant

School emails.
Clear summaries in Russian.

School Mail Bot prepares detailed Russian summaries of selected Gmail messages and sends a daily report to a configured family group on Telegram.

How the bot works

  1. The mailbox owner grants read-only Gmail access through Google OAuth.
  2. The bot selects messages from configured senders received during the previous 24 hours.
  3. OpenAI summarizes each message in Russian, highlighting requests, dates and amounts from the original email.
  4. Report preparation starts at 09:00 each day in the Nicosia time zone.
Gmail access is read-only. Google's permission technically covers the entire mailbox; the bot applies the restriction to selected senders in its own code.

For one family

This application is for personal use by the mailbox owner and their family. There is no public registration or self-service connection of other people's mailboxes.

Attachments are listed by filename; their contents are not analyzed. Important information should be checked against the original email.

Your data and support

Before connecting your mailbox, read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use. For access and deletion requests, contact the bot owner in the family group. The support email address is also shown on Google's consent screen.
